My Appointments

View, update, or cancel your appointment using your reference number.

How it works

Enter the reference number you received when you booked (for example MB-00012) and the email you used.

Status meanings: Pending means waiting for the clinic, Confirmed means your slot is reserved, Completed means your visit is done, and Cancelled frees the slot.

Updates appear on this website only; we do not send SMS or email notifications. Changing the date or time sets your appointment back to Pending until the clinic confirms it.
